Before you sign up

We only accept care students

Musubi does not recruit students at large. Every visiting student is enrolled on a recognised elderly-care, welfare, nursing or rehabilitation programme — and we confirm that with their school before they are given access to anything.

1
Upload two IDs

A government photo ID and a current student ID card, submitted in the app.

2
We verify with the school

We confirm directly with the institution that the applicant is an officially enrolled student on an approved programme.

3
Only then does the app open

Until that check passes there is no access — no browsing, no requests, no addresses.

Students aged 18 and over enrolled in elderly care, care welfare, social welfare, nursing, rehabilitation or a closely related subject.

      Safety system

      Trust, built into every visit

      Someone is coming into a home. We treat that seriously — nobody gets in without being verified by their school, the address is held back until they are on their way, and every visit is opened and closed at the door.

      Before they can use musubi at all

      Two IDs, uploaded

      Every applicant submits a government photo ID and a current student ID card. There are no anonymous accounts anywhere on the platform.

      Verified with the school

      We contact the institution directly to confirm the applicant is an officially enrolled student on an approved care, welfare, nursing or rehabilitation programme.

      No access until it passes

      Until that verification comes back, the app stays locked. No browsing, no requests, no profiles, no addresses.

      On the way & at the door

      The address stays hidden

      A student only ever sees the neighbourhood. The exact address is released at the moment they set off for the visit — never before.

      Live location, 10 minutes out

      Location sharing starts ten minutes before the student is due to arrive, so both the household and the family can see them approaching.

      QR scan starts the session

      A temporary code appears on the older person’s device. Nothing begins until the student scans it at the door — so a visit can only start if the right person is really there.

      During & afterwards

      Family notified, visit closed

      The scan tells the family the visit has started. A second scan on the way out timestamps the end, so its length is recorded rather than self-reported.

      Visit report & hours record

      The check-in the family asked for is delivered automatically, and the verified minutes are added to the student’s hours record for their programme.

      Ratings, blocking & reporting

      Both sides rate each other after every visit, and either can block or report the other. Reports are reviewed by a person, not a queue.
