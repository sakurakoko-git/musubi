    Safety system

    Trust, built into every visit

    Someone is coming into a home. We treat that seriously — every visit is verified at the door, tracked while it happens, and closed out properly.

    Before you meet

    ID verification

    Both sides are identity-verified before they can use musubi at all — no anonymous accounts anywhere on the platform.

    Pre-visit call

    Before a first visit the two of them have a short call — a chance to hear each other’s voice, and for either side to say no without awkwardness.

    At the door & during

    QR check-in

    A temporary code appears on the elderly person’s device. The student scans it at the door, so a visit can only start if the right person is really there.

    Family notified

    The moment that code is scanned the family gets a notification that the visit has started. Nobody has to remember to text.

    QR check-out

    A second scan on the way out closes and timestamps the visit, so its length is recorded rather than self-reported.

    Afterwards

    Visit report

    The student fills in the check-in the family asked for, and it is delivered automatically — no chasing, no relaying messages.

    Ratings, blocking & reporting

    Both sides rate each other after every visit, and either can block or report the other. Reports are reviewed by a person, not a queue.
